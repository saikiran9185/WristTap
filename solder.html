Solder WristTap, one step at a time

No breadboard. Two header-pin strips become the power hubs, resistors sit right in the wires, and every sensor gets tested before the next one. About 40 joints, roughly 2 hours.

Progress: 0 of 10 steps

Rules for every joint

Power OFF while solderingUnplug the USB cable and the power bank every time the iron comes near.
Tin firstStrip 8 mm, twist the strands, touch solder to the wire until it's shiny. Then joining takes 2 seconds.
Heat the metal, not the solderHold the iron on the joint 2–3 s, feed solder into the joint. Shiny and smooth = good; grey and lumpy = reheat.
Tape every jointNo heat shrink, so wrap each joint in its own piece of insulation tape. No bare metal may touch another.
Test after each sensorPlug in, run the check, then unplug. Then you always know which joint broke.
Never use VINVIN (bottom-left pin) is 5 V and kills the sensors. Only 3V3 and GND.
