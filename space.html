  1. Rest your elbow on the same spot every time (desk edge or armrest). The elbow is the anchor.
  2. Keep still for 2 s after starting. The band learns its gyro offset.
  3. Put your hand flat, straight in line with the forearm, on your home mark (a sticker on the desk works).
  4. Set 000 here. Now move: the dot follows your hand.
  5. If x drifts slowly, go back to the home mark and set 000 again.
