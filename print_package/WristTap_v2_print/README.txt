WRISTTAP v2 - PRINT FILES
=========================
Thanks for printing this! It's a sensor wristband: a box on the forearm, a small
shell on the back of the hand, and pods that slide onto velcro straps.

UPDATE 17 Nov evening: the FOREARM BOX and its LID got bigger (bigger breadboard).
If you already started the old version: the pods and the hand shell are unchanged,
keep them. Only reprint the box + lid: ONLY_box_and_lid_BambuA1.3mf (3 h 26 min).

FASTEST (Bambu Lab A1, or any Bambu printer):
  Open  WristTap_v2_ALL_PARTS_BambuA1.3mf  -> everything is already on one plate.
  Print time about 4 h 45 min, about 171 g PLA, NO supports.
  (Other Bambu printer? Studio will ask to switch printer: say yes and re-slice.)

ANY OTHER PRINTER: use the STL folder. The number at the end of each name is how
many to print. All parts are already turned the right way up (flat side on the bed).

SETTINGS
  Material   PLA (any colour)
  Layer      0.16 mm (0.20 is fine too)
  Walls      3
  Infill     20 %
  Supports   OFF. The slots and tunnels are designed to bridge without supports.
  Brim       auto (only the big box may need it)
  Scale      100 % - do NOT scale, the parts must fit real electronics.

PLEASE CHECK
  - The lid slides into the box grooves from the open (short, lower) end and clicks.
    If it's too tight, a light sanding of the lid edges fixes it.
  - Don't break off the thin springy tongue at the lid end: it is the latch.
