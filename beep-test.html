Disc beep test

The ESP32 plays a beep through the disc. Hear it = the disc, its wires and D32 all work. Only one wire moves from the tap test: the disc's red wire goes from i6 to g9.

Connect (USB unplugged)

FromTo
ESP32 GND (left row, 14th pin, above VIN)right blue line 1
ESP32 D32 (left row, 6th pin)f9
Disc red wireg9  (moved from i6)
Disc black wireright blue line 8

The two resistors (faded) can stay where they are. They don't stop the beep.

Never use VIN (bottom pin, 5 V).

Then

  1. Plug in USB.
  2. Tell Claude "yes, upload the beep".
  3. Hold the disc near your ear, or lay it on the table.
Beep every second✅ Disc, wires and D32 work
SilenceDisc wire joint broken, or D32 wire not reaching row 9
