MPU #1: small 4-wire test

The MPU stays in a3–a10. Four wires go straight from the ESP32 to column c. No red/blue lines, no AD0 wire.

What the test result means

0x68 (chip id 0x68) on the correct line✅ MPU and ESP32 both work. The old wiring was the problem.
0x68 only on the swapped lineYellow and blue are swapped. Swap them.
Both wires CONNECTED, but nothing answeredMPU pins not touching (press it sideways), or the MPU is faulty.
A wire says NOT connectedThat wire is on the wrong pin or hole, or loose.
